Ori and the Will of the Wisps VR includes these parts. Their licences are in the licences folder.

OpenXR loader 1.1.65 (Khronos Group), unmodified
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenXR-SDK-Source
  Apache-2.0.

MinHook (Tsuda Kageyu), compiled into OriWotWVRNative.dll
  https://github.com/TsudaKageyu/minhook
  BSD 2-Clause. Includes Hacker Disassembler Engine 64 (Vyacheslav Patkov), BSD 2-Clause.

Dear ImGui 1.93.0 (Omar Cornut), compiled into OriWotWVRNative.dll, draws the settings menu
  https://github.com/ocornut/imgui
  MIT.

Fonts, unmodified
  Alegreya Sans (Juan Pablo del Peral, Huerta Tipografica) and Marcellus SC (Brian J. Bonislawsky)
  https://fonts.google.com
  SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The sharpening pass follows the idea of AMD FidelityFX Contrast Adaptive Sharpening (MIT licence).
It is this mod's own code (from Ori VR by the same author), not a copy of AMD's.

The way into the game (a winhttp.dll proxy and native IL2CPP calls) follows the idea of the Ori and the Will of the
Wisps Randomizer client (https://github.com/ori-community/wotw-rando-client, MIT). No code was copied from it.

Ori and the Will of the Wisps belongs to Moon Studios and Xbox Game Studios. This mod contains no game files.
